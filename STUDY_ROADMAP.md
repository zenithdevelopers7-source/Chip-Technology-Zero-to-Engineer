# Study Roadmap

## Month 1
Semiconductors, electricity, transistors, binary numbers,
logic gates, and basic circuit simulations.

## Month 2
Adders, registers, clocks, memory, and digital circuits.

## Month 3
Verilog, RTL modules, testbenches, and simulation.

## Month 4
CPU architecture, instruction sets, and RISC-V.

## Month 5
AI accelerators, matrix multiplication, and MAC units.

## Month 6
Design, simulate, test, and document a small
AI arithmetic accelerator.
