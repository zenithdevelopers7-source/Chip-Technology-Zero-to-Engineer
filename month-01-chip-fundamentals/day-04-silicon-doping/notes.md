# Day 004 - Silicon and Semiconductor Doping

## Learning Objectives

Understand why silicon is important for computer chips.
Learn the difference between intrinsic, N-type, and P-type silicon.

## Silicon

Silicon is a semiconductor element with four valence electrons.
Silicon atoms form covalent bonds in a crystal structure.

## Intrinsic Semiconductor

Intrinsic silicon has no intentional dopant impurities.
Electrons and holes can both contribute to conduction.

## Doping

Doping is the intentional addition of impurity atoms
to change a semiconductor's electrical properties.

## N-type Silicon

Common dopant: phosphorus.
Electrons are the majority charge carriers.

## P-type Silicon

Common dopant: boron.
Holes are the majority charge carriers.

## Why This Matters

Doped semiconductor regions are fundamental to
diodes, transistors, and integrated circuits.
